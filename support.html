05 / Good to know

A few quick answers

Some simple details before you choose an option.

05 — 05
Is supporting completely optional?

Absolutely. There is no pressure to contribute. Visiting a project, sharing it with someone, or sending feedback is already appreciated.

Can I choose any amount?

Use the amount options available on the payment platform you choose. For UPI, enter an amount in your payment app after scanning the QR code or copying the UPI ID.

Can I support without using a payment platform?

Yes. You can explore my projects on GitHub, share something you find useful, or report an issue. Those contributions help too.

What happens after I contribute?

Thank you! Your support is appreciated and helps encourage future independent work. Payment processing and receipts are handled by the payment option you use.

One last thing

Thanks for being here.

No pressure, ever. Whether you support, share, give feedback, or simply take a look around, I'm glad you stopped by.
